About Sandali

A Thoughtful Approach to Language Education

Language is more than a subject to learn. It is a way to communicate ideas, explore perspectives, and connect with the world.

Sandali Ashara is an English language and literature educator, English language lecturer, and teacher trainer with a professional interest in language education, literary studies, and teacher development. Her work reflects a broad engagement with English language teaching and learning across diverse educational contexts.

Areas of Expertise

Where Language Meets Possibility

Language opens doors to new perspectives, meaningful connections, and endless opportunities for growth. Through English language education, literature, academic communication, and teacher development, the aim is to empower learners and educators to communicate confidently, think critically, and embrace lifelong learning.
