About Sandali

A Thoughtful Approach to Language Education

Language is more than a subject to learn. It is a way to communicate ideas, explore perspectives, express creativity, and connect with the world.

I am Sandali Ashara, an English Language and Literature Educator, Lecturer, and Teacher Trainer passionate about creating meaningful learning experiences. My professional journey is shaped by a deep interest in language, literature, pedagogy, and the evolving landscape of education.

I view education as a continuous process of discovery, where teaching and learning complement one another. My approach embraces reflective practice, activity based language learning, and innovative pedagogical strategies that encourage individuals to become confident communicators, independent thinkers, and lifelong learners.

Beyond teaching, I am interested in exploring educational ideas, questioning conventional practices, and transforming meaningful insights into practical learning experiences. I believe that effective education should not simply equip learners with knowledge but inspire them to think, create, communicate, and see the world from different perspectives.

Learning, thoughtfully.

Volunteer Engagement

Creating Meaningful Community Impact

AMERICAN CORNER MATARA | DYNED PROGRAMME

English Language Learning through Cultural Engagement

I contributed to an activity-based English language camp for the young bhikkhus of Migadaya Pirivena, Matara, through the DynEd programme conducted in collaboration with American Corner Matara. The experience was particularly rewarding as I witnessed the young monks enthusiastically engaging with English through interactive activities, demonstrating how language learning can transcend cultural, religious, and generational boundaries.

USBUS – MOBILE AMERICAN SPACE | U.S. EMBASSY SRI LANKA

Find Your Voice: Public Speaking for University Students

As a volunteer facilitator, I contributed to a public speaking development session for undergraduates of the University of Ruhuna, organised through USBus – Mobile American Space, an initiative of the U.S. Embassy in Sri Lanka. The session focused on building students' confidence, strengthening their communication skills, and encouraging effective public expression. Witnessing their enthusiasm, active participation, and willingness to step beyond their comfort zones made the experience especially meaningful.
