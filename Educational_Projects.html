Educational Projects & Resources

Practical, learner-centred approaches that turn language learning into something active, interactive and memorable.

Explore this work ↓
Learning designed around the learner, not the textbook.

Good resources do not just deliver content. They give learners a reason to speak, a space to try, and the confidence to try again.

These projects bring together interactive classroom activities, educational technology and carefully designed learning materials. Each one starts with a simple question: what will help this group of learners communicate more confidently?

The result is a growing collection of speaking tasks, group activities, digital tools and learner-friendly resources that support English language development at university, vocational and community level.

What the work includes

A

Interactive classroom activities

Games, role plays and communicative tasks that get learners using English in real situations rather than only studying it.

T

Educational technology

Digital tools and online platforms used thoughtfully to extend practice beyond the classroom and keep learners engaged.

R

Learner-centred resources

Worksheets, activity packs and guides written for the learners in front of her, at the right level and in plain language.

F

Feedback and reflection

Simple ways for learners to notice their own progress, and for the teacher to adjust the next lesson accordingly.

How a project takes shape

Understand

Learn who the learners are, what they need and what holds them back.

Design

Build an activity or resource around one clear communication goal.

Facilitate

Run it, observe closely and support learners as they try.

Reflect

Gather feedback, refine the material and use it again.
